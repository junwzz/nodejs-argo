🤖 What is AI really?

Human-like intelligence

AI is a field of computer science focused on building machines that can think, learn, and solve problems — just like humans (but faster with data).

Core concept

Learning from data

Instead of hard-coded rules, AI learns patterns from examples: images, text, voice, or numbers. More data = smarter AI!

Machine learning

Everyday intelligence

From Netflix recommendations to voice assistants — AI quietly works behind the scenes, making your devices helpful and intuitive.

You use it daily

AI that learns from data without explicit programming.
🧠 Neural Network
A computer system inspired by the human brain.
💬 LLM
Large Language Model (like ChatGPT) that understands and generates text.
🎯 Algorithm
Step-by-step instructions for solving a problem.
